# THE TELL — commercial validation plan

## Status and guardrails

THE TELL is a free virtual-chip entertainment/decision game. A useful challenge-edition fulfillment candidate is now built and tested, but the paid offer remains **proposed, not available for purchase**. No checkout, merchant fulfillment operation, sale, customer, revenue, profit, or validated willingness-to-pay has been observed. Actual revenue remains **unverified: $0 observed**.

There are no deposits, purchased chips, prizes, cash-outs, rake, gambling referrals, or promises of returns. Real-money poker is a separate gated product and is not part of this release.

## Audience and offer hypothesis

Audience hypothesis: adults who enjoy short strategy games, poker decision practice, cinematic browser games, and replayable AI opponents without real-money wagering.

Proposed offer: a **$9 one-time entertainment edition**. The finished candidate currently contains:

- three distinct, tested opponent profiles with disclosed tendencies and behaviorally different pressure thresholds/sizing;
- three bounded decision-action challenges, with local progress and a clear reset/delete control;
- opponent switching that starts a fresh virtual-chip match;
- a complete 13-file allowlisted offline-capable package, local licensed vendor code, and written start instructions.

Not built: additional rooms and durable per-decision history. Those are optional future scope, not current product claims. Checkout, delivery automation, support contact/terms and an active refund policy also remain external commercial gaps.

The price is a test choice, not evidence of demand. The free browser table stays genuinely playable; the paid concept will not sell chip refills, randomness, cash value, or competitive advantage.

## Demand test

1. Publish and browser-verify the free demo.
2. Show a plainly labeled “proposed $9 edition” note and link only to a feedback channel—never a buy button or fake checkout.
3. Ask whether the listed finished content would be worth $9 and which feature matters most.
4. Count only unique, intentionally submitted responses. A page view, click, social like, or proposed price is not revenue or a sale.
5. Build and verify the promised package before enabling any checkout.
6. Only inspect an already-approved merchant account if one is available. Do not create accounts, accept financial/legal terms, transfer money, or publish checkout without owner-approved fulfillment and authorization.

Minimum decision rule for further work: evidence of repeated specific interest in the same deliverable, not a vanity traffic threshold. Record response counts and verbatim themes without inventing conversion metrics.

## Fulfillment, support, and refund outline

Proposed fulfillment: immediate download/access to a versioned static offline package after a genuinely completed payment, plus a checksum and concise install instructions. Support must state a monitored contact and response target before checkout goes live. Proposed refund policy: clearly displayed before purchase, consistent with the approved storefront and applicable consumer law; defects or inability to access the promised package require a straightforward remedy. No policy is active until an authorized storefront and final terms are verified.

## Unit economics

Use completed-payment records only:

`net revenue = completed gross payments - refunds - chargebacks - payment/store fees - taxes collected/remitted`

`contribution = net revenue - attributable delivery/support costs`

`profit = contribution - attributable development, legal, compliance, hosting, and marketing costs`

| Completed payments | 0 verified | Not forecast yet |
| Gross revenue | $0 verified | $9 × completed sales |
| Refunds / chargebacks | $0 observed | Unknown |
| Fees / taxes | $0 observed | Use approved provider schedule only |
| Net revenue | $0 verified | Formula above |

## Real-money legal and security gates

Real-money play remains blocked unless all of the following are independently approved and implemented: eligible jurisdiction and legal review; operator/vendor licensing; age verification, KYC/AML, sanctions controls and geofencing; responsible-gambling limits and self-exclusion; approved acquiring/payment processing; certified RNG/fairness; anti-collusion and fraud controls; secure server-authoritative game state; audited ledger and funds custody; privacy/security review; tax/reporting; and staffed dispute/support operations. A static client-side game cannot satisfy these gates.

## Current blockers

- `checkout_blocked`: no verified authorized merchant checkout or delivery operation.
- `fulfillment_candidate_built`: the versioned static package is locally generated and browser-tested, but is not published as a paid download.
- `revenue_unverified`: no completed payment is observable.
- `real_money_blocked`: legal, licensing, KYC/geofencing, payment, server-security, audit, and responsible-gambling approvals are absent.
